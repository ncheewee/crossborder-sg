What the pink line is adding up

Woodlands SG→JB. Google Maps times the road. Checkpoint.sg times the crossing. The pink shadow fit is the translation between them.

1 · Two clocks on the same trip
BKE pin GMaps starts
Road queue Maps sees stop-go traffic
Plaza + booths CIQ and stacking, mostly invisible to Maps
Cleared Checkpoint.sg finish

GMaps is driving time along a mapped road from the BKE pin to a clearance pin. Checkpoint.sg is “how long will the whole crossing take?” — the number drivers treat as truth.

2 · Three pieces, then a small fudge

Pink minutes ≈ booth floor + hidden queue + today’s leftover − 5.

Intercept = 10m Empty causeway still needs CIQ. This is counter time that exists even when Maps is nearly free-flow.
+
Slope × GMaps = 1.9 × road When the approach slows, the plaza is worse than the road implies. Each extra Maps minute is a proxy for ~1.9 minutes of real crossing.
+
Bias Today’s leftover vs Checkpoint.sg after the two terms above: closed booths, holiday, rain. Learned slowly, then fades.
−
Minus 5 min Start-line fudge. Our BKE pin is south of Checkpoint’s clock, so we shave 5 so we don’t over-count the approach.
3 · Same idea as a graph

If Maps were truth, the dashed 1:1 line would be the crossing. It never is — booths exist even on an empty road, so the real line starts at 10 minutes, not zero.

Slope 1.9 means the line is steeper than 1:1. Road congestion is a leading indicator of plaza overflow Maps cannot fully see. 20 minutes on Maps is not “20 plus booths”; it is closer to 10 + 1.9×20.

Bias is the vertical gap between that purple line and Checkpoint.sg right now. The pink line rides the purple line, then nudges toward today’s Checkpoint residual (slowly on SG→JB, faster on JB→SG).

4 · One 11:00 worked example (SG→JB)

GMaps approaches 18 / 25 / 24 min · mean 22

  1. Booth floor: 10
  2. Hidden queue: 1.9 × 22 = 42
  3. Static base: 10 + 42 = 52
  4. Start-line fudge: 52 − 5 = 47
  5. Today’s bias (~+3 to +10 after learning): pink lands around 46–59 per approach

Checkpoint.sg midpoint that hour was 58. Pink is allowed to lag it — SG→JB only takes 25% of each residual, so a sudden Checkpoint jump does not yank the line all the way in one slot.
